How it works: seven phases, one at a time

Paste the prompt, fill in your topic, field and level. The AI works through one phase per answer, ends with a checklist and waits until you are ready for the next one.

  1. 0

    Sharpen the question

    A searchable research question (PICO or another fitting framework), a concept table with synonyms and thesaurus terms, include/exclude criteria.

  2. 1

    Get access

    VPN, institutional login or library proxy, explained for your system. No university? Legal open-access routes instead.

  3. 2

    Set up Zotero

    Install Zotero and the browser connector, with exact menu paths for metadata, PDF retrieval and your citation style.

  4. 3

    Build the search

    Database recommendations for your field and a ready-to-paste search string for each one. With web search on, strings for open databases are tested with real hit counts.

  5. 4

    Import and screen

    A collection and tag scheme for Zotero, then step-by-step import and screening against your criteria.

  6. 5

    Cite in Word

    Zotero's Word plugin, your first citation and a reference list in APA 7 or any style you choose.

  7. 6

    Wrap up

    A final checklist and a short summary of your search for the methods chapter.

Is it for you?

Made for

  • Bachelor's and master's theses, seminar papers, PhD chapters
  • Systematic and scoping reviews that need a documented search
  • Any field: psychology, health, sport, education, business and more
  • Students with or without university database access
  • Answers in your language, references in your citation style

Not made for

  • Writing your thesis for you: reading and judging stay with you
  • Getting around paywalls: only legal routes, no shadow libraries

What you get

  • The full prompt, as a version for ChatGPT or for Claude

Questions

Is this allowed at my university?

That depends on your institution's rules on AI use. The prompt reminds you to check them and to disclose AI use where required. It never queries licensed databases for you and never downloads paywalled PDFs.

Does it work without university access?

Yes. Enter "none" as your university and the prompt switches to open databases and legal routes such as open access, public libraries and interlibrary loan.

Which citation styles are supported?

APA 7th Edition by default. You can set any other style, for example Harvard, Chicago, MLA or IEEE, and Zotero formats your reference list in it.

ChatGPT or Claude version?

Buy the version for the chatbot you use. Both follow the same 7 phases and rules. Turn on web search so the AI can check thesaurus terms and test search strings.

Do I need Zotero experience?

No. Phase 2 walks you through installing and setting up Zotero with exact menu paths.

Does the AI download paywalled PDFs for me?

No. You search licensed databases yourself. The prompt never suggests shadow libraries and points you to legal ways in: open-access versions, interlibrary loan or emailing the author.

Will it write my thesis?

No. It plans and documents your literature search. Reading, judging and writing stay with you.
